# Labelling guideline: agent_drafts

A sentence, question or item is **supported** when a careful human reader, holding only the candidate's confirmed facts, the
company's dossier claims and the campaign's events, would sign it. It is **unsupported** when it:

1. states a number, date, skill, tool, employer, title or outcome the cited items do not state (layer 1 and layer 2);
2. makes a bigger claim than the cited items ("led" for "contributed to", "ran the whole platform" for "led a team");
3. cites an alias that does not exist, or nothing at all, while saying something checkable;
4. contains an email address, a link, a phone number, pay, a notice period, a start date or availability;
5. in a Writer packet, answers pay, notice, start date or work authorisation (those four are always the candidate's);
6. in a Coach pack, tells a story with no fact (a gap may have none, but then it may claim nothing), tests an unknown
   requirement, or offers a question to the company that rests on no dossier claim or misstates one.

A courtesy sentence ("Thank you for your time") with nothing checkable in it is supported with no citation. A sentence that
cites a dossier claim in a packet that was given no dossier is unsupported.

Labels written by an agent carry `"labels": "agent-drafted, maintainer review pending"` until a maintainer has reviewed
them; a second person should label 20% of the items independently.
